Northstar: make complex work feel clear

A shared space to plan, write, review, and move work forward with confidence.

◷   Last edited 10:42 AM▣   Planning✓   Saved

What we are building

Northstar brings the project’s notes, decisions, and deliverables into one familiar workspace. People can find a file, open it beside a conversation, and see what has changed without losing their place.

Working principle
Keep the main work visible. Make help available nearby, and ask before applying consequential changes.

Success looks like

  • New contributors can find the current brief and next step quickly.
  • Every suggested change is visible, reviewable, and reversible.
  • Work resumes where a person left it, across sessions and devices.
